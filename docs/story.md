# About

The story is simply background flavor that adds context to the core gameplay.

## Story

You wake up in a traditional indonesian village home. Dark. Simply lit sparsely with candles stood on wooden plates. The walls are made of wood and roof with starch. The floor is the dirt right from the outside, as if they just put walls in the middle of a field.

An old woman stands in the doorway leading into more of the house, which you can't see. They didn't seem to put candles beyond this room. After staring at you for a while, the old woman sits down on a chair behind the only table in the otherwise empty room. She calmly but firmly gestures for you to sit on the other side.

On the table is a single candle and a new game of Congklak.
